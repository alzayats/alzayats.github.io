Research Fellow · Computer Vision & Deep Learning

Alzayat Saleh

James Cook University, Australia

I build computer vision systems that work outside the lab: in murky water, in bright paddocks and in clinical scans. Much of my work asks how to train reliable models when labelled data is scarce, using self-supervised, semi-supervised and weakly supervised learning, and how to make those models small and fast enough to deploy on real equipment.
